The 40 • 15 • 3 Loop

Standing all day causes foot strain. Sitting all day ruins your posture. The secret is continuous posture rotation.

🦐

1. Sitting Focus

40 min

Lower your desk. Sit upright with relaxed shoulders. Focus on deep engineering or writing without distractions.

🧍

2. Standing Desk

15 min

A gentle chime and modal prompts you. Elevate your desk. Hit Start and continue coding with 100% free screen space.

🚶

3. Active Break

3 min

Step away from the screen entirely. Stretch your spine, grab a glass of water, and recharge your eyes.
